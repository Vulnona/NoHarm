The University of Potsdam and the Hasso Plattner Institute are jointly responsible for data processing. In accordance with Art. 26 GDPR they have concluded an agreement determining their respective responsibilities for compliance with GDPR obligations.

Purpose of the processing

Data are processed to conduct the study “Ethical Preferences in the use of Machine Learning (ML) for Medical Triage Decisions”. Canvas fingerprints are recorded and stored for the duration of the data collection to prevent participants from answering more than once. IP addresses are recorded and stored for the duration of the survey to determine the current country of residence.

Lawfulness of processing

The legal basis for data processing is your consent to the processing of special categories of personal data (health data and data revealing religious or philosophical beliefs) according to Art. 6 para. 1 sentence 1 lit. a GDPR in conjunction with Art. 9 para. 2 lit. a GDPR.

Withdrawal of consent

You have the right to withdraw your consent at any time and without reason. The withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing based on consent before its withdrawal.

Type of data

All data containing information about your responses to the survey will be stored, including your chosen patients to treat, fairness ratings, and information about gender, age, religion, health, and family planning. Additional health information, canvas fingerprints (screen resolution, timezone, operating system and device type, browser version, installed plugins and fonts, cookies, user agent string, hardware properties, mouse and keyboard input), and IP addresses are temporarily stored until data collection is complete.

Duration of data processing

Canvas fingerprints and IP addresses are deleted after the last survey has been completed. Upon deletion, the link between device identifiers and participants will no longer be available. Your data will not be shared with third parties or other recipients within the Hasso Plattner Institute or the University of Potsdam.

Your rights

You have the right to obtain confirmation whether your personal data is being processed and to access the data and related information (Art. 15 GDPR). You may obtain rectification of inaccurate personal data without undue delay (Art. 16 GDPR). Where the legal requirements of Art. 17 or 18 GDPR are met, you may request erasure or restriction of processing. Please note that restricted processing may not be possible in every instance.

You have the right to receive your personal data in a structured, standard, machine-readable format or to request the transfer to another controller (Art. 20 GDPR). Furthermore, you may object to the processing of your personal data under the conditions of Art. 21 GDPR.
